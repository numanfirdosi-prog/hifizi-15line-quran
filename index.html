عن نور القرآن • PLATFORM INTEGRITY

Nūr Al-Quran (نور القرآن)

A high-fidelity, production-grade 15-Line Islamic Quran platform developed to preserve the time-honored Pakistani and Ottoman Hifz tradition in a modern, distraction-free digital sanctuary.

📖

📜 The 15-Line Hifz Tradition

In the 15-line Mushaf standard, every single page begins and ends cleanly at a complete Ayah boundary (Aayat-un-Nihayah). Exactly 20 pages comprise one Juz (Para), allowing Huffaz across the world to maintain flawless photographic spatial memory during memorization and Taraweeh recitation.

✔ Verified Data Sources

All Quranic text, diacritics, and verse numbering are verified against the King Fahd Complex for Printing of the Holy Quran (Mujamma' al-Malik Fahd) and the Tanzil Project. No text is generated or synthesized.

خصوصية وأمان • PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Sanctuary

100% Local-First. Your spiritual study, personal reflections, and reading coordinates belong entirely to you.

🛡 Zero Telemetry & Local Storage Guarantee

Nūr Al-Quran does not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or telemetry beacons. Every bookmark, highlight, private note, and reading position is stored strictly inside your browser's private sandbox (IndexedDB and localStorage).

📶 Network Communication

External network requests occur solely to download official Quranic text chapters and audio recitation chunks from public CDN servers. No personal identifiers or reading metrics are transmitted in these requests.

🗑 Data Deletion & Total Reset

You retain permanent, unconditional authority over your local data. You can export your data at any time via the Backup page, or completely erase all records with one click.
